Chapter I

The Spark of Us.

Dosti aksar shor mein hoti hai, par hamari dosti us khamoshi mein shuru hui jise sirf do gehre dil samajh sakte hain. Betma ki un galiyon se lekar aaj ke is pal tak, har lamha ek Hifazat karne layak memory ban gaya hai.

Chapter II

Moments in Amber.

Yaad hai wo baatein? Log kehte hain main chup hoon, par unhe nahi pata ki tumhare saath ki wo khamoshi bhi kitni dilchasp hai. Humne dooriyon ko sirf ek Geographical distance mana, dosti ki gehraayi ko nahi.

Chapter III

Unspoken Truths.

Tum aksar kehti ho, "Suyog, tumhe mujhse behtar mil jayegi." Par Nenu, tum ye kyu nahi samajhti ki "Behtar" ki talash wo karte hain jinko "Asli" ki pehchan nahi hoti. Mere liye tumse behtar koi nahi, sirf tum hi kafi ho.

Chapter IV

Gratitude.

Shukriya mera support system banne ke liye. Shukriya ye ehsas dilane ke liye ki boring hona bura nahi hai, agar aapke paas ek sahi insaan ho jo aapke chup rehne ki wajah bhi samajhta ho.

Finale

Beyond Words.

Ye khat kabhi khatam nahi hoga, kyunki hamari dosti waqt aur faslon se pare hai. Main hamesha wahi rahunga—tumhari respect karta hua, tumhara khayal rakhta hua.

— From Suyog, who values you beyond words.
